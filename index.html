About

I am Lubna Zahan Lamia, a PhD student in Computer Science at Dartmouth College. My research interests broadly span natural language processing, human–AI interaction, and responsible AI, with a particular interest in understanding how large language models generate, present, and mediate information in real-world settings. My recent work has examined model behavior related to meaning, framing, perspective, and sentiment, with the goal of better understanding how AI-generated information can influence human interpretation and trust. More broadly, I am interested in developing evaluation and analysis methods that improve the transparency, reliability, and human-centered design of AI systems.
